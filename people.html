Principal investigator

Prof. Chitraleema Chakraborty

Assistant Professor of Materials Science and Engineering, with a joint appointment in Physics and Astronomy, University of Delaware. NSF CAREER awardee.

Prof. Chakraborty's research aims to understand solid-state quantum emitters for quantum information processing and nanoscale sensing. Her group combines computational materials science with experimental quantum optics to predict, fabricate and image quantum emitters in 2D materials and hybrid quantum systems, and to integrate them with photonic and nanomechanical devices.

She received her Ph.D. in Materials Science from the University of Rochester in 2018, a dual M.S./M.Tech. in Nanophysics and Nanostructures from the University of Delhi and Université Joseph Fourier in Grenoble, and a B.Sc. (Honors) in Physics from Jadavpur University in Kolkata in 2009.
