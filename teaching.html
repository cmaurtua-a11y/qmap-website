Outreach

Research experiences for students

QMAP hosts undergraduate and high school researchers every year through programs such as UDRAW, UD SOURCE, the UD Summer Scholars program, NSF REU sites and CHARM. Students work on real projects in 2D materials and optics alongside graduate mentors, and some end up as co-authors: CHARM REU student Alexander Hutchinson is an author on the group's 2026 hBN preprint.
